Better Together: Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Better Together is a private app used by two people (a household) to organise their days, plans, health, money and home. It is not offered to the public.

What the app stores

The things you enter in the app (for example habits, journal entries, to-dos, expenses and settings) are stored in the app's database so they sync between your devices and, where shared, with your partner. Your partner can see your data in the app; nobody else can.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the app asks Google for permission to view and edit events in your calendars (calendar.events) and to see your email address. This is used only to show your events in the app's Plan tab and to add, change or delete events when you ask it to. A Google access token is kept on the app's server so this keeps working; it is never shared with anyone else or used for anything else. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time in the app (Plan → Google Calendar → Disconnect), or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app stores a notification address for your device so it can send you reminders and updates.

No selling or advertising

Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

You can erase your data in the app (Settings → Your data → Erase everything), or ask the app's owner to delete your account.
